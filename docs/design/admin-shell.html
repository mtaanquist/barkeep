Barkeep · Back Bar · the shell

The queue is the floor. Everything else is a drawer.

Five peer tabs was the bug. Pending orders is not a tab — it is the app. The other four are setup, grouped, demoted, and named as a group. Two shells carry this: a rail at ≥1024, and a bottom queue bar below it. In both, the queue is a full-width surface of its own, which is also why the navigation survives Afventende bestillinger at 390 — the long label never sits in a row of peers.

Three rules the whole shell obeys

01 · ASYMMETRY

The queue gets an ink-filled surface, the largest type in the chrome, and a live count. Setup gets a mono group label, muted 15px rows, no fills. You should be able to tell which one is the job from across the room, out of focus.

02 · THE COUNT IS NEVER GONE

Rail tile or bottom bar, the pending count is on screen on every route including the drink form, and tapping it is always one gesture back to the queue. Nothing in this app is allowed to render over it or in place of it.

03 · ARRIVAL IS THE ONLY ACCENT

Yellow appears in the chrome for exactly one reason: an order arrived while you were elsewhere. A 3px accent edge and an accent count chip, held until the queue is next opened. Not a toast, not a sound, not a redirect — the bartender's hands are busy.

1280 · rail, holding the queue

232px rail, fixed, never scrolls. Reading down: identity (bar name, role) — the queue tile, 88px tall, ink fill, count set in 32px mono — the setup group under a mono kicker, four 48px rows, muted, no fill, active marked by a 2px ink left edge and ink text — then the footer: QR always visible, language and logout tucked into a menu. The content column owns its own title and primary action; the rail never repeats them.

The drink form is a sub-screen. Not a modal, not a slide-over.

Modal, rejected: it is the longest form in the product, with three sections and a sticky save bar of its own — a dialog is for a decision you can hold in your head, and this isn't one. A backdrop over the shell also dims the count, which rule 02 forbids. Slide-over, rejected: at 390 it is a full screen wearing a costume, and at 1280 a 640px panel over a dimmed list buys context nobody uses while typing a recipe. Sub-screen, chosen: it gets a real URL (/admin/drinks/42), a linkable one, a labelled back that names where back goes, and it keeps the shell — rail on the left at 1280, queue bar at the foot below 1024. The dialog component stays, and now has exactly one job in the admin: destructive confirmation.

URLs, the back button, and where the global actions live

ROUTES · BACK BEHAVIOUR
URLBACK GOES TO
/adminRedirects (replace) to /admin/queue, always. The remembered-tab behaviour is deleted on purpose: after a break you land in the queue, not in Analytics.
/admin/queueOut of the app. It is the root — there is nowhere further back.
/admin/drinks
/categories · /analytics · /settingsReal history entries (push). Back retraces the tabs you actually visited; from the first setup screen you opened, back returns to the queue.
/admin/drinks/new
/admin/drinks/:id/admin/drinks. If the form is dirty, back is intercepted once: the destructive dialog asks Kassér ændringer? and back from that dialog only closes the dialog. Hard navigation gets beforeunload.
?qr=1A pushed query on whatever route you're on, so back closes the QR overlay and leaves you where you were. Escape and the backdrop do the same.
errorsNever a route. A failed save renders inside the form with the URL untouched, so back still means “leave the form”; a dropped socket is the connection banner over the content, not a page. Only a hard 404 on :id replaces the form with an empty state whose one action is Tilbage til drinks.
GLOBAL ACTIONS · VISIBLE OR TUCKED
QR-kodeVisible, every width, every route. It is a party action, not setup — a guest arrives, you show your screen. Rail footer at 1280, labelled button at 820, 48px icon at 390. Opening it goes full-bleed: a white card behind the code even in dark mode, and the bar name under it so the guest knows what they scanned.
Barens navnVisible, small. Identity, not an action — 19px at the top of the rail, 17px at 390. Editing it lives in Settings; the shell only ever displays it.
SprogTucked. Set once, at the start of the night. It sits in the rail footer line / the ⋮ menu, and is also a proper field in Settings. Two entrances, one truth.
Log udTucked, and confirmed. Never a bare button in the header: logging out mid-party while the queue is live is unrecoverable in the moment. Behind the account line, with a dialog naming the count — “Der er 9 afventende bestillinger.”
THE LONGEST LABELS, MEASURED
nav.queue — “Pending orders” / “Afventende bestillinger” (24 ch). Full-width bar below 1024; wraps to two lines by design in the 232px rail. Never truncates, never scrolls.
nav.setup.* — longest is “Indstillinger” (13 ch) vs “Settings” (8). The 4-up row at 820 gives each 197px; at 1280 they are rows, so length is free.
nav.group — “Setup” / “Opsætning”, mono 10px, never constrained.
action.qr — “Show QR code” / “Vis QR-kode” (11 ch). At 820 the label drops to “QR-kode”; at 390 the label drops entirely and the icon is the button.

What the shell changes in the screens we already designed

The rule that settles most of it: the shell owns identity and location; the screen owns its title, its live numbers and its one primary action. A screen title is not a duplicate of a nav label — the nav says where you are, the title says what you're looking at, and the title is the only one allowed to carry counts that move.

Respec: the Tabs component is retired

“Tabs — bartender top-level navigation” in the kit encoded the wrong idea: five equal things in a row that scrolls when it doesn't fit. Its horizontal-scroll behaviour was a symptom, and the fix isn't a scroll shadow. Three components replace it.

NAVRAIL · ≥1024

232px fixed. Slots: identity, QueueTile, setup group, footer. Props: barName, pending, hasNew, route. Active setup row = 2px ink left edge + 600 weight; never a fill, because fill is reserved for the queue.

QUEUEBAR · <1024

64px, sticky bottom, full-bleed. Two states, one component: on-queue (surface, live dot, no chevron — a status strip) and away (ink fill, back chevron, tappable). Third modifier hasNew: 3px accent top edge, accent count chip. Nothing may render above it.

SETUPNAV · <1024

Exactly four items, never more, so it never needs to scroll — that is the spec, not a layout. ≥600: a 4-column grid, equal widths, 52px, active underlined 2px. <600: a labelled Opsætning disclosure that drops a 64px-row sheet from the header. A fifth setup screen would break this; if one ever exists, it goes inside Settings.

Deleted from the kit: the five-peer tab bar, its horizontal-scroll variant, and the count badge's use on a tab. The badge component survives but is respec'd to one use — the queue count, at 26–32px mono, never as a 16px dot on a label.
